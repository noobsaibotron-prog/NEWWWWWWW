OBJECTIVE (NEW — a STABILITY goal, not a single class)
------------------------------------------------------
  V2 baseline (post-persistence-fix, measured on the SAVED+RELOADED blob, repro=ok on all 12 seeds):
  at the bundle config (thinPosWeight=2.0, Resonance threshold 0.225, 600 ep, lr 0.003), a single
  deterministic blob passes the all-8 C1 gate on only **4/12 init seeds**; 8/12 fail on ASSORTED
  classes (Reso/Thin/Sib/Mud/clean-FP). Everything seed-varies (Thin 17->45, Reso 28->50, Sib 32->50,
  clean-FP 0->25). => The blocker is TRAINING INSTABILITY w.r.t. the init seed: the SAME recipe lands
  a different, often-failing model depending on the random init.
  GOAL: lift the seed pass-rate (4/12 -> most/all) so ANY seed yields a shippable blob — explicitly
  NOT "pick a lucky seed" (rejected repeatedly), NOT more threshold tuning.

WHAT IS ALREADY TRIED (and why it doesn't serve THIS objective)
---------------------------------------------------------------
  - AB2 weight-decay (classic L2 regularization): NEGATIVE — suppressed Thinness + inflated clean-FP.
    Naive L2 is OUT.
  - AB1 mini-batch averaging at FIXED lr: under-trained (the MEAN step shrank per-epoch movement ~B x).
    The idea (variance reduction) is RIGHT; the execution (no lr compensation) was WRONG.

LEVER 1 — candidate (counter-exam picks ONE)
--------------------------------------------
  (A, RECOMMENDED) lr-COMPENSATED mini-batch averaging = variance reduction done right. Average the
      gradient over a mini-batch of B samples AND preserve the per-epoch step (apply the SUM, i.e.
      effective lr = lr*B; or mean + lr scaled by B). This lowers per-step SGD NOISE — the source of
      seed-trajectory divergence — WITHOUT under-training (fixes the AB1 failure mode directly).
      Sweep B in { 1 (control), 4, 8, 16 }. Default B=1 == current (byte-identical). DROP-IN on
      ml_weights.bin (no architecture change).
  (B, alternative) DROPOUT on the problem-net hidden layers — the untried CLASSIC regularizer. Needs
      train/eval modes (bigger surface); on a tiny 128->64 net dropout can be aggressive. Sweep p in
      {0 (control), 0.1, 0.2}. Also drop-in on the weight file (dropout is train-only).
  RATIONALE for recommending A: the measured failure is seed-VARIANCE; lr-compensated mini-batch
  attacks the variance most directly, and AB1 already built/validated the (reverted) infrastructure.
  Classic regularization (weight-decay) already failed, so A is the more targeted "stability" lever.
